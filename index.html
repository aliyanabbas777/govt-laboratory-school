About Our School

Govt. Laboratory Model Higher Secondary School is committed to supporting students in their educational journey. Our aim is to encourage learning, develop knowledge and skills, and help students prepare for a bright future.


We believe that education helps students grow academically, socially, and personally. We encourage students to develop curiosity, confidence, responsibility, and a love of learning.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide quality education and help every student develop knowledge, confidence and good character.

🌟 Our Vision

To inspire students to become responsible, creative and successful lifelong learners.

🤝 Our Values

We believe in respect, honesty, discipline, teamwork and equal learning opportunities.
